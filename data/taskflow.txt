TaskFlow Support Guide

Inviting teammates
Workspace administrators can invite teammates from Settings > Members > Invite.
Enter the teammate's email address and select Send invitation.
Invitations expire after seven days. An administrator can send a new invitation.

Resetting a password
Select Forgot password on the sign-in screen and enter your account email.
The password reset link expires after 30 minutes.

Exporting tasks
Open a project, select its menu, and choose Export CSV.
Only workspace administrators can export tasks.

Cancelling a subscription
An administrator can cancel from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription.
Access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Support hours
Email support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 UTC.
